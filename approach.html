METHODOLOGY

Structured delivery with defined outcomes

Each engagement follows a documented process designed to reduce delivery risk, maintain compliance alignment, and ensure client teams can operate delivered systems independently.

01

Discovery and assessment

Review of current architecture, security controls, operational processes, and compliance obligations. Outputs include a threat model, risk register, and prioritised recommendations.

02

Architecture and design

Target-state architecture with network boundaries, identity flows, data residency constraints, and control mappings to applicable frameworks. Design is reviewed with client security and engineering stakeholders before build.

03

Implementation

Incremental delivery using infrastructure-as-code and version-controlled change practices. Changes are reviewed and deployed through defined pipelines with policy gates where required.

04

Handover and support

Runbooks, architecture documentation, and training sessions for client teams. A defined post-implementation support period is included in transformation engagements. Ongoing retainer arrangements are available where required.

Principles

Security by design

Security controls are incorporated at the architecture stage, not added after deployment. Network segmentation, identity management, and logging requirements are treated as core design inputs.

Documented change control

Infrastructure and application changes are traceable. We favour version-controlled, policy-as-code approaches in environments where audit trails and approval workflows are mandatory.

Client ownership

The objective of implementation work is for client teams to operate and maintain delivered systems. We avoid creating dependencies on proprietary tooling or undocumented configuration.

Scope discipline

Work is delivered against agreed statements of work. Changes to scope, timeline, or deliverables are documented and approved before additional effort is incurred.

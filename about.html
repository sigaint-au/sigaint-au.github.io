ABOUT

Australian infrastructure consultancy

Sigaint provides infrastructure and security consulting to enterprise organisations operating under Australian regulatory and sovereignty requirements.

Who we are

Sigaint Pty Ltd is an Australian-owned consultancy with consultants across Australia. We work with clients across the public and private sectors on platform engineering, security architecture, and operational resilience.

Our work focuses on environments where compliance, auditability, and data residency are operational requirements. This includes systems subject to ISM controls, Essential Eight assessments, Privacy Act obligations, and government cloud frameworks.

What we do

We advise on and implement infrastructure platforms, network security, automation workflows, and supporting security controls. Engagements range from short discovery assessments through to full platform delivery and ongoing retainer support.

  • Architecture review and threat modelling
  • Platform design and implementation
  • Security control integration and compliance mapping
  • Documentation, training, and operational handover

How we operate

We work as an extension of client engineering and security teams. Deliverables are documented, scope is defined before commencement, and knowledge transfer is included in every implementation engagement. We do not retain operational control of client systems beyond agreed support periods.

Client environments and engagement details are treated as confidential. We do not publish client names or production system details without explicit written consent.

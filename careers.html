Work on substantive infrastructure problems

Sigaint hires experienced engineers and security practitioners for client-facing consulting roles. We prioritise depth of technical knowledge and clear written communication.

Working at Sigaint

Consultants at Sigaint work across a range of client environments, typically involving container platforms, identity systems, network security, and automation tooling. Engagements are project-based or retainer-based, with most delivery conducted remotely and periodic onsite work at client facilities.

We maintain a small core team and engage specialist contractors where specific expertise is required. All team members are expected to produce documentation suitable for handover to client engineering staff.
